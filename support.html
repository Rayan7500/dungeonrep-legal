Frequently asked

The camera doesn't count my push-ups. Place your phone on the floor so it can see your upper body and arms, in good lighting. Grant camera access in iOS Settings ▸ Dungeon Rep.

How do I restore my purchase? Open Profile (or the Premium screen) and tap “Restore purchases”.

Will my progress sync across devices? Sign in with Apple to carry your progress; guest progress stays on the device.

How do I manage or cancel Premium? iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions.

Health & safety

Dungeon Rep is for entertainment and general fitness. Warm up, use good form, and stop if you feel pain. Consult a doctor before starting any exercise program.
